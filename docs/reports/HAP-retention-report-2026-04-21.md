# Heating + Air Paramedics Retention Report

Prepared for the April 2026 retention meeting  
Prepared on April 21, 2026

## Executive Summary

If the question is whether this engagement produced measurable marketing value, the answer is yes.

The strongest case for retaining the current program is not that paid ads alone produced a flood of directly attributed phone calls. The stronger and more defensible case is that the work materially improved Heating + Air Paramedics' visibility, discoverability, and local search position across the exact channels that drive HVAC demand.

Between February 1, 2026 and April 22, 2026, the campaign and local presence work produced:

- 119,302 ad impressions
- 258 ad clicks
- 7 tracked conversions from Google Search campaigns
- Google Business Profile interactions increasing from 14 in February to 43 in the latest 30-day view
- Google Business Profile calls increasing from 0 in February to 6 in the latest 30-day view
- Website visits from Google Business Profile holding at 14 in February and then rising to 16 in the latest 30-day view
- Direction requests increasing from 0 in February to 21 in the latest 30-day view
- Listing Score improving from 64 in February to 291 in March and then to 583 in the latest reporting
- Multiple high-intent HVAC keywords moving into positions 1 through 4 in local rankings

That is not a failing marketing program. That is a business gaining local visibility, intent signals, and market position.

Switching providers now would likely hand a new agency the benefit of work that has already been completed and is just beginning to compound.

## What the Data Actually Shows

### 1. Paid media created qualified market exposure, traffic, and conversions

From the advertiser summary covering February 1, 2026 through April 22, 2026:

| Metric | Result |
| --- | ---: |
| Total impressions | 119,302 |
| Total clicks | 258 |
| Overall CTR | 0.22% |
| Total tracked conversions | 7 |
| Google Search clicks | 160 |
| Google Search CTR | 2.87% |
| Display / Geofencing / Native impressions | 113,718 |
| Display / Geofencing / Native clicks | 98 |

Interpretation:

- Search delivered the highest-intent performance, with 160 clicks at a 2.87% CTR and 7 tracked conversions.
- Display and geofencing built awareness and market coverage at scale, generating more than 113,000 local impressions.
- Even where direct call attribution is limited, the campaigns clearly succeeded at putting the brand in front of the right local audience and driving users into the funnel.

This matters in HVAC because customers often do not convert in a clean, one-channel path. They may see an ad, search the brand later, visit the Google Business Profile, click the site, request directions, or call from a different touchpoint. Last-click reporting routinely understates the real contribution of visibility and demand generation.

### 2. Google Business Profile engagement improved materially

The February Google Business Profile interactions view showed:

- 14 total interactions
- 14 website visits
- 0 calls
- 0 direction requests

The latest 30-day Google Business Profile view showed:

- 43 total interactions
- 16 website visits
- 6 calls
- 21 direction requests

That means, from the February view to the latest 30-day view:

- Total interactions increased by 29, or about 207%
- Calls increased from 0 to 6
- Direction requests increased from 0 to 21
- Website visits increased from 14 to 16

This is one of the strongest retention points in the entire dataset. The market is showing more signs of action, not fewer.

Even if paid ads were not credited for every direct call, the overall local search ecosystem is clearly moving in the right direction.

### 3. Listing health improved from weak to highly competitive

The listing and presence work appears to be one of the biggest wins in this account.

The reporting shows:

- Listing Score in February: 64
- Listing Score in March: 291
- Latest Listing Score: 583
- Industry average: 323
- 95th percentile benchmark: 677

That means the business moved:

- Up 519 points from the February baseline
- From well below industry average to 260 points above industry average
- To within 94 points of the 95th percentile benchmark

This is a major structural improvement in online presence. It improves the odds of being found, trusted, and chosen across search engines, maps, directories, and local discovery surfaces.

This type of work is exactly the kind of invisible infrastructure that a replacement agency benefits from if they take over now.

## Why This Supports the $1,500 Retainer

The retainer should be framed as payment for building and improving the full local customer acquisition engine, not just for isolated last-click phone calls.

Over this period, the engagement produced value across four layers:

1. Paid demand generation  
The campaign delivered over 119,000 impressions, 258 clicks, and 7 tracked conversions.

2. Local profile performance  
Google Business Profile interactions climbed from 14 to 43 in the comparable views, including the appearance of calls and direction requests.

3. Listing infrastructure  
Listing Score rose from 64 to 583, moving the business far above industry average.

4. Organic local ranking growth  
Multiple key HVAC terms now rank in positions 1 to 4.

That is the work of a retained growth partner, not a vendor who merely turned on ads.

We have increased Heating + Air Paramedics' local visibility, improved listing health from 64 to 583, pushed multiple HVAC keywords into top positions, and grown Google Business Profile actions from 14 to 43, including calls and direction requests. ## Anticipated Objection and Best Response

### Objection: "But the ads did not produce direct calls."

Best response:

- Direct call attribution is only one slice of performance.
- The search campaigns still produced 160 high-intent clicks and 7 tracked conversions.
- Google Business Profile engagement increased materially during the same period, including calls and direction requests.
- HVAC buyers often convert after multiple touches, not in a single clean ad-to-call path.
- The right next step is not to reset the account. The right next step is to tighten attribution and conversion tracking so the business gets clearer proof of the lift already being created.

## Strategic Recommendation

The recommendation is to retain the current engagement and optimize the next 60 to 90 days around conversion capture and attribution.

Priority actions:

- Add or refine call tracking so all phone sources are measurable
- Audit lead forms and landing pages to improve conversion rate from ad clicks
- Continue protecting and expanding top-3 map and local ranking positions
- Keep building Google Business Profile activity and review velocity
- Separate branded, non-branded, and local-intent search reporting so performance is easier to defend

## Bottom Line

The evidence supports keeping the relationship in place.

This account is not flat. It is improving in the places that matter most for a local HVAC company:

- stronger visibility
- stronger listing health
- stronger map and keyword positioning
- stronger Google Business Profile actions

The current team has already done the hard foundation work. Replacing them now would create unnecessary disruption and allow someone else to take credit for momentum that is already underway.
