# Shark Branding Solutions Website Brief

## Goal
Rebuild `www.sharkbrandingsolutions.com` around a product-led funnel that positions **The Business App** as the flagship experience, uses the **AI Visibility Scan** as the entry point, and converts users into the **AI Visibility Toolkit**.

This should feel closer to an Apple-style product site than a traditional agency site.

## Core Strategy

### What the site is selling
- Primary product: **The Business App**
- Entry point: **AI Visibility Scan**
- Core activation layer: **AI Visibility Toolkit**
- Retention and engagement layer: **The House of Sharks**

### What the site is not
- Not a generic marketing agency site
- Not a feature dump
- Not a pricing-led SaaS site
- Not a services menu

### Positioning
- `Everything your business needs to thrive in an AI-driven world`
- `Branding was the beginning. AI is the future.`
- `The Business App shows you what’s happening. The AI Visibility Toolkit makes sure you show up.`
- `We show you where your business stands — and give you the system to improve it.`

## Brand Architecture

### Keep for now
- **Shark Branding Solutions** remains the parent brand

### Do not launch yet
- Do not front-face a full `Shark AI` rebrand yet
- Do not replace the main company name sitewide yet
- Do not announce a full brand change all at once

### Introduce later
- `Shark AI` can be introduced later as a product division or innovation layer after the product experience is established

## Primary User Journey
1. Visitor lands on homepage
2. Learns that customer choice is shifting because of AI
3. Takes the **AI Visibility Scan**
4. Gets access to **The Business App** while the scan is being prepared
5. Begins exploring the system
6. Receives scan results
7. Understands what is missing and what needs to be activated
8. Converts into **AI Visibility Toolkit**
9. Joins **The House of Sharks** for ongoing engagement, education, and retention

## Main Navigation

### Keep simple
- Home
- The Business App
- Case Studies
- AI Resources
- About
- Contact

### Primary CTA
- `Get Your Free AI Visibility Scan`

### Remove for now
- Pricing
- AI App Store as a main nav item
- Workshops as a primary nav priority on the homepage-focused build

## Homepage Purpose
The homepage should function as a **guided product funnel**, not as a broad agency overview.

It should move users from:
- curiosity
- to scan request
- to Business App access
- to toolkit activation

## Homepage Structure

### Section 1: Hero
Purpose:
- Immediate clarity
- Product-led framing
- Primary CTA

Content:
- Headline: `Everything your business needs to thrive in an AI-driven world`
- Subhead: `Introducing the Business App — your AI-powered system to run, manage, and grow your business in one place.`
- CTA: `Get Your Free AI Visibility Scan`
- Supporting line: `Your scan takes up to 48 hours. While you wait, you’ll get access to your Business App.`

Layout:
- Left: headline, subhead, CTA
- Right: product visual / MacBook / system preview

### Section 2: The Shift
Purpose:
- Establish urgency
- Reframe market behavior

Content:
- Headline: `The way customers choose businesses has changed`
- Short explanatory copy about AI scanning, filtering, and recommending businesses

Layout:
- Clean centered text block

### Section 3: AI Visibility Scan
Purpose:
- Present the first action
- Connect uncertainty to diagnosis

Content:
- Headline: `Start by seeing where you stand`
- Explain that the scan shows where the business appears, where it is missing opportunities, and why competitors may be getting chosen instead
- CTA: `Get Your Free AI Visibility Scan`

Layout:
- Left: copy + CTA
- Right: simple report or results preview

### Section 4: Bridge / 48-Hour Access
Purpose:
- Turn the waiting period into immediate value
- Introduce the product without friction

Content:
- Headline: `Don’t wait to take control`
- Explain that while the scan is being built, access to The Business App is unlocked immediately
- Reinforce `No setup. No guesswork.`

Layout:
- Highlighted section
- Should feel like a differentiator

### Section 5: Product Reveal
Purpose:
- Introduce The Business App as the flagship system

Content:
- Headline: `The Business App`
- Subhead: `One place to run and grow your business with AI`
- Supporting lines:
  - `See everything.`
  - `Manage everything.`
  - `Improve everything.`

Layout:
- Strong product reveal
- Apple-like simplicity
- Use existing product visuals

### Section 6: What You Can Do Inside
Purpose:
- Show product value through outcomes, not feature jargon

Content blocks:
- `See how your business is showing up`
- `Stay connected to every opportunity`
- `Know what’s working`
- `Keep your business accurate everywhere`

Layout:
- Grid of 4 outcome cards

### Section 7: Positioning Shift
Purpose:
- Create a realization moment

Content:
- Headline: `Seeing your business is one thing. Being chosen is another.`
- Supporting copy: `Most businesses have tools. Very few have a system that actually gets them recommended.`

Layout:
- Bold, centered, minimal

### Section 8: AI Visibility Toolkit
Purpose:
- Introduce the core monetization layer
- Make it feel essential, not optional

Content:
- Headline: `Turn on what actually drives visibility`
- Subhead: `The AI Visibility Toolkit`
- Explain that this is what powers how the business is understood, trusted, and recommended
- Key line: `The Business App shows you what’s happening. The AI Visibility Toolkit makes sure you show up.`

Layout:
- Slightly elevated visual treatment
- Can include a simple ecosystem or connected system visual

### Section 9: How It Works
Purpose:
- Add one more layer of clarity around why the toolkit matters

Content:
- Headline: `How your business actually gets chosen`
- Explain that AI is constantly scanning content, listings, activity, and consistency
- Explain that if those signals are not clear, connected, and trusted, the business gets overlooked
- Show that the AI Visibility Toolkit changes that

Layout:
- Simple educational section
- No technical jargon

### Section 10: Community
Purpose:
- Add retention, support, and social proof without distracting from the main funnel

Content:
- Headline: `You’re not doing this alone`
- Copy: `While your AI Visibility Scan is being prepared, you’ll get access to The House of Sharks — a private network where businesses and their teams connect, share, and improve how they show up and get chosen in an AI-driven world.`
- Supporting bullets:
  - Learn what’s working right now
  - Share wins and get feedback
  - Connect with other businesses and teams
  - Start improving your visibility before your report is ready

Layout:
- Homepage support section
- Not a main competing CTA

### Section 11: Final CTA
Purpose:
- Close clearly

Content:
- Headline: `Start with your AI Visibility Scan`
- Supporting copy:
  - `See where you stand.`
  - `Get access to your Business App.`
  - `Take control of how your business gets chosen.`
- CTA: `Get Your Free AI Visibility Scan`

- In sales and onboarding conversations

- The AI Visibility Toolkit is the flagship app or activation layer inside the system
- It should not feel optional

## Community: The House of Sharks

### Role
The House of Sharks is the network layer around the product experience.

It should feel like:
- a digital Chamber experience
- a business network
- a learning and results environment

It should not feel like:
- a static course
- a support desk
- a random Facebook group

### Community tagline
`The House of Sharks`

`Where businesses connect, share, and improve how they show up and get chosen in an AI-driven world.`

### Community access
- Give access while the AI Visibility Scan is being prepared
- Position it as early access, not as a waiting room
- If content is still light, frame it as early access to a new network

### Community structure
- Community Feed
- Events & Networking
- Wins & Results
- Ask & Optimize
- Start Here
- Trainings

### Community purpose
- engagement
- networking
- education
- proof
- retention

## Visual Direction
- Apple-like simplicity
- Product-led
- Minimal, intentional, premium
- Large type, strong spacing, fewer competing concepts
- Avoid generic agency stock-photo feel
- Product visuals should feel like a system, not a dashboard template

### Brand color guidance
- Stay inside the existing Shark Branding Solutions brand palette
- Use the current blue and cyan / aqua family as the primary accent system
- Use dark navy foundations and pale blue-gray light sections
- Do not introduce orange or unrelated conversion colors
- Keep accent usage restrained and consistent across CTAs, signals, and product visuals

## Copy Rules
- Clarity over cleverness
- Outcomes over features
- Product language over service language
- Do not over-explain technical mechanics
- Do not lead with Vendasta
- Do not use too many abstract terms at once

## What to Avoid
- Too many top-level concepts in navigation
- Too much AI jargon
- A service-menu homepage
- Pricing-heavy framing
- Overbuilt explanations of how the system works technically
- Too many CTAs competing with the scan

## Final Summary
This rebuild should feel like:
- a flagship AI-powered business product
- with a clear entry point
- a guided experience
- a monetization layer inside the system
- and a community that keeps people engaged

The homepage should not feel like an agency site with product language layered on top.

It should feel like a product ecosystem with one clear first action:

`Get Your Free AI Visibility Scan`
